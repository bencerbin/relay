A little context

How Relay works

Relay helps families connect with community programs that support whole-child health: nutrition, physical activity, healthcare, and social-emotional well-being. Share what your family needs in your own words, and Relay will ask a few focused questions before showing local options.

Relay is a community connection tool, not a substitute for emergency or medical care.
